### The transitions of a chain are never the powers of one map

This is the [DeansProblem.Chain.Powers][] module of the [agda-lattices][] library.

[the paper]: https://arxiv.org/abs/YYMM.NNNNN

After Corollary A3, [the paper][] remarks that no chain is generated by one
embedding, even with a period, and adds: "the maps `θₖ` of Definition 4.1 are
never the powers of one map."  The first claim, that no injective endomorphism
`Φ` of `FL(X)` carries every link onto the link `p` places later, is Corollary
A3 at the bottom link (`no-periodic-embedding`{.AgdaFunction} of
[DeansProblem.Chain.Condition][]).  The second does not follow from Corollary
A3, since `Φ` acts on `FL(X)` and `θₖ` on `FL(3)`.  It follows from Lemma 6.1
instead, the bound on the transitions from the bottom
(`transition-bound′`{.AgdaFunction} of [DeansProblem.Coherent][]), and this
module proves it so.

**The statement**.  Let `c` be a chain over generators with decidable
equality, with steps `θₖ` (the terms `terms k` of the record
`Chain`{.AgdaRecord} of [DeansProblem.Chain][], which are the `θₖ` of
Definition 4.1).  There is no self-map `ψ` of `FL(3)` with `θₖ = ψ^(mₖ + 1)`
for every `k` (`not-powers`{.AgdaFunction}); the form for a chain of copies of
`FL(3)` (`CopyChain`{.AgdaRecord} of [DeansProblem.Chain.Copies][]), whose
`θₖ` are the terms `copy-terms`{.AgdaFunction}, follows
(`not-powersᶜ`{.AgdaFunction}).  The constant case, `θₖ = ψ` for every `k`, is
`mₖ = 0`.  (A zero power is excluded already, since each `θₖ` misses a
generator and the identity misses none.)

**The proof**.  Suppose `θₖ = ψ^(mₖ + 1)`.  Then the transition from the
bottom to link `k`, the composite `θ_{0,k}` of the first `k` steps, is the
power `ψ^(Mₖ)` with `Mₖ = Σ_{e<k} (mₑ + 1)` (`transition≈power`{.AgdaFunction}),
and `M` is strictly increasing.  By Lemma 6.1 the transitions from the bottom
take finitely many values, so `θ_{0,j} = θ_{0,k}` for some `j < k`
(`transition-repeats`{.AgdaFunction}), that is, `ψ^(Mⱼ) = ψ^(d + Mⱼ)` with
`d = Mₖ - Mⱼ ≥ 1`.  The transition `θ_{0,j}` is injective, as a first factor of
the injective `gens 0`, and so is the power `ψ^(Mⱼ)` equal to it; cancelling it
gives `ψ^d(xᵢ) = xᵢ` for each generator (`power-fixes`{.AgdaFunction}).  Then
every generator is `ψ` of something, hence equal to some `ψ(xⱼ)` by Corollary
2.4 (`generator-in-image`{.AgdaFunction}; `gen-in-ψ`{.AgdaFunction}), so every
generator lies in the image of every power of `ψ`
(`gen-in-power`{.AgdaFunction}), in particular in that of `θ₀ = ψ^(m₀ + 1)`;
and Corollary 2.4 once more makes the generator that the bottom step misses one
of its terms, against the record.  In short: the powers of a proper
self-embedding are pairwise distinct, while Lemma 6.1 allows the transitions
from the bottom finitely many values.

**No Theorem H in scope**.  Theorem H refutes every chain, so a statement whose
hypothesis is a chain would hold by the absurdity of the hypothesis if Theorem
H were in scope.  This module imports neither [DeansProblem.NoChain][] nor
[DeansProblem.LengthThree][], directly or through another module; the proof is
the one above.

#### The transitions are powers

A sequence of natural numbers that increases at every step increases strictly
(`step→strict`{.AgdaFunction}).  Fix a chain whose steps are powers of `ψ`,
`θₖ = ψ^(mₖ + 1)`.  The exponent of the transition from the bottom to link `k`
is `Mₖ` (`exponent`{.AgdaFunction}), which increases at every step, and the
transition is that power of `ψ` (`transition≈power`{.AgdaFunction}), by
induction on `k`: the transition over `k + 1` steps is the one over `k`
followed by `θₖ`, and powers add (`[]-iter-+`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
-- A sequence that increases at every step increases strictly.
step→strict : (f : ℕ → ℕ) → (∀ k → f k < f (suc k)) → ∀ {j k} → j < k → f j < f k
step→strict f step {j} {suc k} (s≤s j≤k) = split (m≤n⇒m<n∨m≡n j≤k)
  where
  -- Either j < k, or j is k.
  split : j < k ⊎ j ≡ k → f j < f (suc k)
  split (inj₁ j<k)   = <-trans (step→strict f step j<k) (step k)
  split (inj₂ refl)  = step j

module _ {X : Type χ} (_≟ˣ_ : DecidableEquality X) (c : Chain X) (ψ : Sub (Fin 3) (Fin 3)) (m : ℕ → ℕ)
         (pow : ∀ k i → Chain.terms c k i ≈ʷ iter ψ (suc (m k)) i) where
  open Chain c

  -- The exponent of the transition from the bottom to link k.
  exponent : ℕ → ℕ
  exponent zero     = zero
  exponent (suc k)  = exponent k + suc (m (k + 0))

  -- The exponents increase strictly.
  exponent-strict : ∀ {j k} → j < k → exponent j < exponent k
  exponent-strict = step→strict exponent (λ k → m<m+n (exponent k) (s≤s z≤n))

  -- The transition from the bottom to link k is the power ψ ^ exponent k.
  transition≈power : ∀ k i → transition c 0 k i ≈ʷ iter ψ (exponent k) i
  transition≈power zero     i = ≈ʷ-refl
  transition≈power (suc k)  i =
    ≈ʷ-trans ([]-cong (terms (k + 0)) (transition≈power k i))
      (≈ʷ-trans ([]-congʳ (iter ψ (exponent k) i) (pow (k + 0)))
        (subst (_≈ʷ iter ψ (exponent (suc k)) i)
               ([]-iter-+ (ℊ i) ψ (exponent k) (suc (m (k + 0)))) ≈ʷ-refl))
```

#### A power of ψ fixes the generators

The transition `θ_{0,j}` is injective, as a first factor of the injective
`gens 0`, and so is the power of `ψ` equal to it
(`power-injective`{.AgdaFunction}).  The repeat of Lemma 6.1
(`transition-repeats`{.AgdaFunction}), read through the exponents, says that
`ψ^(Mⱼ)` and `ψ^(d + 1 + Mⱼ)` agree on the generators, where
`Mₖ = d + 1 + Mⱼ` (`repeat-cancels`{.AgdaFunction}); cancelling `ψ^(Mⱼ)`
leaves `ψ^(d + 1)(xᵢ) = xᵢ` (`power-fixes`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
  -- The power equal to the transition from the bottom to link j is injective.
  power-injective : ∀ j → IsInjective (proj₁ (subst-hom (iter ψ (exponent j))))
  power-injective j {s} {t} e =
    first-factor-injective (transition c 0 j) (gens (j + 0)) (gens 0) (gens≈transition c 0 j)
      (freelyGenerates→injective (gens 0) (gens-free 0))
      (≈ʷ-trans ([]-congʳ s (transition≈power j))
                (≈ʷ-trans e (≈ʷ-sym ([]-congʳ t (transition≈power j)))))

  -- A repeat of the transitions, with the later exponent d + 1 past the earlier.
  repeat-cancels : ∀ j k d → suc d + exponent j ≡ exponent k
                 → (∀ i → transition c 0 j i ≈ʷ transition c 0 k i)
                 → ∀ i → iter ψ (suc d) i [ iter ψ (exponent j) ] ≈ʷ ℊ i [ iter ψ (exponent j) ]
  repeat-cancels j k d Mk≡ e i =
    subst (_≈ʷ iter ψ (exponent j) i)
          (trans (cong (λ n → iter ψ n i) (sym Mk≡)) ([]-iter-+ (ℊ i) ψ (suc d) (exponent j)))
          (≈ʷ-sym (≈ʷ-trans (≈ʷ-sym (transition≈power j i)) (≈ʷ-trans (e i) (transition≈power k i))))

  -- Some positive power of ψ fixes every generator.
  power-fixes : Σ[ d ∈ ℕ ] (∀ i → iter ψ (suc d) i ≈ʷ ℊ i)
  power-fixes =
    let (j , k , j<k , e) = transition-repeats _≟ˣ_ c
        (d , Mj+d≡Mk) = m≤n⇒∃[o]m+o≡n (exponent-strict j<k)
    in d , λ i → power-injective j
                   (repeat-cancels j k d (trans (cong suc (+-comm d (exponent j))) Mj+d≡Mk) e i)
```

#### The generator the bottom step misses

If `ψ^(d + 1)` fixes the generators, every generator `xᵢ` is `ψ` of the term
`ψ^d(xᵢ)`, so equal to some `ψ(xⱼ)` (`gen-in-ψ`{.AgdaFunction}, by Corollary
2.4); then every term `t` is `ψ` of the term `t` with each generator replaced
by such a preimage (`ψ-onto`{.AgdaFunction}), so every generator is the value
of a term under every power of `ψ` (`gen-in-power`{.AgdaFunction}), and under
the bottom step `θ₀ = ψ^(m₀ + 1)`.  Corollary 2.4 makes it one of the terms of
`θ₀`, which for the generator the bottom step misses is false
(`powers→⊥`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
  private
    -- The exponent of the power that fixes the generators, less one.
    d : ℕ
    d = proj₁ power-fixes

    -- Every generator is ψ of some generator.
    gen-in-ψ : ∀ i → Σ[ j ∈ Fin 3 ] ℊ i ≈ʷ ψ j
    gen-in-ψ i = generator-in-image ψ (iter ψ d i)
                   (≈ʷ-sym (subst (_≈ʷ ℊ i) ([]-iter-sucʳ (ℊ i) ψ d) (proj₂ power-fixes i)))

    -- A preimage under ψ of each generator.
    pre : Sub (Fin 3) (Fin 3)
    pre i = ℊ (proj₁ (gen-in-ψ i))

    -- Every term is ψ of a term.
    ψ-onto : (t : LatTerm (Fin 3)) → t [ pre ] [ ψ ] ≈ʷ t
    ψ-onto t = subst₂ _≈ʷ_ ([]-⨟ t pre ψ) ([]-ℊ t) ([]-congʳ t (λ i → ≈ʷ-sym (proj₂ (gen-in-ψ i))))

    -- Every generator is the value of a term under every power of ψ.
    gen-in-power : ∀ n i → Σ[ t ∈ LatTerm (Fin 3) ] ℊ i ≈ʷ t [ iter ψ n ]
    gen-in-power zero     i = ℊ i , ≈ʷ-refl
    gen-in-power (suc n)  i =
      let (t , e) = gen-in-power n i
      in t [ pre ] , ≈ʷ-trans e (≈ʷ-sym (subst (_≈ʷ t [ iter ψ n ]) (sym ([]-iter-suc (t [ pre ]) ψ n))
                                               ([]-cong (iter ψ n) (ψ-onto t))))

  -- Steps that are powers of one map contradict the generator the bottom step misses.
  powers→⊥ : ⊥
  powers→⊥ =
    let (t , e) = gen-in-power (suc (m 0)) (fresh 0)
        (j , e′) = generator-in-image (terms 0) t (≈ʷ-trans e (≈ʷ-sym ([]-congʳ t (pow 0))))
    in fresh∉ 0 j e′
```

#### The remark, for chains and for chains of copies

```agda
-- The remark after Corollary A3: the steps of a chain are never the powers of one map.
not-powers : {X : Type χ} → DecidableEquality X → (c : Chain X)
           → ¬ (Σ[ ψ ∈ Sub (Fin 3) (Fin 3) ] Σ[ m ∈ (ℕ → ℕ) ]
                  (∀ k i → Chain.terms c k i ≈ʷ iter ψ (suc (m k)) i))
not-powers _≟ˣ_ c (ψ , m , pow) = powers→⊥ _≟ˣ_ c ψ m pow

-- The same for a chain of copies of FL(3), whose steps θ k are its terms.
not-powersᶜ : {X : Type χ} → DecidableEquality X → (c : CopyChain X)
            → ¬ (Σ[ ψ ∈ Sub (Fin 3) (Fin 3) ] Σ[ m ∈ (ℕ → ℕ) ]
                   (∀ k i → copy-terms c k i ≈ʷ iter ψ (suc (m k)) i))
not-powersᶜ _≟ˣ_ c = not-powers _≟ˣ_ (copyChain→chain c)
```
